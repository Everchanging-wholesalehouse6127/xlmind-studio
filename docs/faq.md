# Frequently asked questions

Short answers. The full FAQ, including licensing, delivery, payment and invoicing, is on the [official support page](https://xlmindstudio.com/en/support?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=github_public_repo&utm_content=faq_support).

## The product

**What is XLMind Studio?**  
A Windows desktop add-in for Microsoft Excel. It adds 153 tools across two ribbon tabs — XLMind Data and XLMind Plus — for data cleaning, text, numbers, dates, comparison, formula auditing, reporting and dashboards.

**Is it a separate application?**  
No. It runs inside Excel. There is no export step and no separate file format.

**Can I build dashboards and reports with it?**  
Yes. Create Dashboard uses ready layouts (Executive, Sales, HR, Finance), KPI Cards provides live single-metric cards, and the Charts and Maps groups add data-bound visuals that stay live.

**Can it combine multiple sheets or tables?**  
Yes. Append Worksheets and Append Selected Tables combine several sources into one table, and Lookup from Table fetches matching data from another table by key.

## System and installation

**Which systems are supported?**  
64-bit desktop Microsoft Excel on Windows. The verified compatibility matrix is published on the [system requirements page](https://xlmindstudio.com/en/system-requirements?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=github_public_repo&utm_content=faq_sysreq).

**Does it work on Mac, Excel for the web or mobile?**  
No. Excel for macOS, Excel for the web and mobile Excel apps are not supported. Neither are 32-bit Office installations.

**Do I need administrator permission to install it?**  
It may be required, depending on Windows and company policies. Corporate devices may need IT approval.

**Does it need a permanent internet connection?**  
No. The tools run on your computer. Internet access may be required for licensing and activation, payment, downloads, update checks, in-product information messages and support.

## Privacy

**Does XLMind Studio upload my spreadsheet data?**  
No. Excel files, cell contents and worksheet names are not sent to XLMind Studio servers during normal product use. Your Excel data stays on your computer for XLMind Studio’s local processing.

**Is my Excel data shared with AI services?**  
No. Excel content is not sent to or shared with artificial intelligence services.

**What data is processed at all?**  
Only the limited data needed for licensing, sales and support. See the [Privacy Policy](https://xlmindstudio.com/en/privacy-policy) and [Personal Data Protection Notice](https://xlmindstudio.com/en/personal-data-protection).

## Trial and licensing

**Is there a free trial?**  
Yes — 7 days.

**Does the trial include all features?**  
Yes. The trial is not a reduced edition. The period starts with the first successful trial activation.

**Does the trial become a paid license automatically?**  
No. No charge is made when the trial ends; buying a license is a separate step.

**How many devices does a license cover?**  
A Single License allows one active device at a time; the 5-license plan allows up to five devices owned or controlled by the license holder. License keys may not be shared or resold.

**What happens when I change computer?**  
Activate on the new computer with your license code. If the device limit is full, the oldest active device is deactivated automatically.

## Downloading and security

**Where should I download XLMind Studio?**  
Only from <https://xlmindstudio.com>. Installers are not published as GitHub Releases and are not distributed through third-party download sites.

**Is the installer digitally signed?**  
Yes, with a code-signing certificate. Publisher details and the SHA-256 checksum of the current release are on the [security page](https://xlmindstudio.com/en/security?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=github_public_repo&utm_content=faq_security).

**Why does Windows show a security warning?**  
SmartScreen weighs reputation as well as the signature, and reputation takes time to build for a recently published release. Verify the publisher and the checksum rather than disabling protection — see [troubleshooting.md](troubleshooting.md).
